Memory Index Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-11-19

Memory Index is a private Android app by SlimeTrail Productions, used by its owner as a personal dashboard.

What the app accesses

The app uses Google sign-in with the Google Sheets scope (read and write) and the Google Drive scope (read-only). It only accesses the owner's own spreadsheet and the app's own update files in the owner's Drive.

How data is used

Data stays on the owner's phone and in the owner's own Google account. Nothing is sold, shared with third parties, or used for ads. There are no ads and no analytics.

Removing data

Uninstall the app and revoke its access at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Contact

Use the developer support contact listed on the app's Google consent screen.
